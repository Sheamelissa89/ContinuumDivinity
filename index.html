Before you begin

A little guidance for your journey

Where should I begin?

I See — Awareness offers a natural starting point. You can also begin with the stage that feels most relevant to your present experiences and return to the others when you are ready.

Do I need all five volumes?

Each volume can be explored independently. The complete collection brings all five stages together for readers who want the wider journey, with room to revisit each stage as their understanding grows.

What is included in my purchase?

Each individual volume includes its guide and workbook together in one downloadable PDF. The complete collection includes all five volumes together in one downloadable PDF. These are digital products; no physical books will be mailed.

How can I use the workbook reflections?

Read the PDF on your device and write your responses in a separate journal, or print the reflection pages for your personal use. Move at your own pace and revisit questions whenever you wish.

Is there a schedule to follow?

There are no deadlines or required pace. Pause when you need to, spend longer with a question, and return when you feel ready.

All products are digital downloads. No physical products will be mailed. Purchases are processed and delivered through Payhip.
