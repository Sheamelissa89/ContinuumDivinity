Information & Policies

Helpful information about your digital purchases and your experience with Continuum Divinity.

✦

Your Purchase

Digital Products

All Return to Yourself guides and workbooks are digital products delivered as downloadable PDF files.

Each individual volume includes its guide and workbook together in one PDF.

The Complete Return to Yourself Collection includes all five volumes together in one downloadable PDF.

No physical books or printed materials will be shipped.

A Special Thank You

The Founding Readers Program

Continuum Divinity began with a vision of personal healing, conscious growth, and collective transformation.

To express appreciation for those who support this vision during its earliest stages, the Founding Readers Program is being developed as a special opportunity for qualifying purchasers of the Complete Return to Yourself Collection.

Who May Qualify?

The program is intended for purchasers of the Complete Return to Yourself Collection who meet both of the following conditions:

  • Purchase the Complete Collection before April 1, 2027.
  • Be among the first 500 qualifying Complete Collection purchases.

The eligibility period will close when either limit is reached, whichever occurs first.

A Future Physical Collection

The intention is to recognize eligible founding readers with one complimentary physical edition containing all five volumes of the Return to Yourself series.

This would be one complete printed collection rather than five separate physical books.

Physical editions are not currently available. Publication dates, production arrangements, shipping, and redemption procedures have not yet been finalized.

The Founding Readers Program is currently a proposed appreciation initiative, not a guaranteed physical product included with a digital purchase.

Additional details will be announced if the program is formally launched.

Checkout & Delivery

Purchasing Your Guides

Purchases are processed through Payhip. When you select a guide or collection on Continuum Divinity, you will be directed to its Payhip product page.

Digital delivery and download access are provided through the checkout platform following purchase.

If you experience difficulty accessing your purchase, please reach out using the contact information below.

Your Experience

Using the Guides & Workbooks

The Return to Yourself collection was created for personal reflection, self-discovery, and exploration.

You may read your guide digitally, record your responses in a separate journal, or print reflection pages for your personal use.

There is no required schedule or pace. You are invited to explore the material in the way that best supports your individual journey.

Purchase Questions

Refunds & Order Support

If you have a question about a purchase, digital delivery, or a refund request, please contact Continuum Divinity.

Requests can be reviewed based on the circumstances of the purchase and applicable consumer protections.

For checkout-related technical difficulties, support may also be available through Payhip.

An Important Note

Personal Growth & Reflection

Continuum Divinity provides educational and reflective materials intended to support personal exploration and growth.

These materials are not a substitute for professional medical, mental health, legal, or financial advice.

Every individual's experiences and circumstances are different. Readers are encouraged to make choices that reflect their personal needs and seek qualified professional support when appropriate.
